🎮  How to Play

  1. 1Click a card to flip it face up.
  2. 2Click a second card to look for its pair.
  3. 3Match: both cards stay open. No match: they flip back after about 1 second.
  4. 4Find all 6 pairs to win. Fewer attempts is a better score.
⚠️ Clicks are ignored on: a third card while two are open, a matched card, and the same card twice.
